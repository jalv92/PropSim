Before you use this

The prop-firm rules in this tool are best-effort and dated. They were read from each firm's own published pages and every value carries the date it was retrieved. Firms change rules roughly quarterly and sometimes contradict their own pages — during research one firm had removed a daily loss limit entirely while its pricing page still showed it, and another's marketing page understated a payout cap by 2.5×.

Verify with the firm before paying for an evaluation. Values the tool could not confirm are marked UNVERIFIED and every account shows what the simulator does not model.

This is a simulator, not financial advice, and a simulated pass rate is not a prediction about your account.
