Before you use this

The prop-firm rules in this tool are best-effort and dated. They were read from each firm's own published pages and every value carries the date it was retrieved. Firms change rules roughly quarterly and sometimes contradict their own pages — during research one firm had removed a daily loss limit entirely while its pricing page still showed it, and another's marketing page understated a payout cap by 2.5×.

Verify with the firm before paying for an evaluation. Values the tool could not confirm are marked UNVERIFIED and every account shows what the simulator does not model.

This is a simulator, not financial advice, and a simulated pass rate is not a prediction about your account.

Setup

Optimizer — search parameter ranges over your own tick data, rank the results, and take the winning values to NinjaTrader. A 200-combination run over 28 sessions takes about four seconds here.

Each range is from:to:step, or a comma-separated list. Leave a parameter blank to hold it at its default.

Take it to NinjaTrader

Paste this into your NinjaScript State.SetDefaults block. The caveats are inside the snippet on purpose — a parameter set copied out of a results table arrives stripped of every qualification unless the qualification travels with it.

Describe a strategy in English and have Claude write it as a PropSim strategy file, or port one you already have to NinjaScript. It runs on your own Anthropic API key and your own bill — PropSim sends the request to api.anthropic.com and nothing else leaves the machine.

A model writing a strategy is not an edge, and this does not pretend otherwise. What it writes is parsed against the plugin allowlist, executed in the restricted namespace, run on your real ticks, and then probed for lookahead: the tape is re-run with prices after a trade changed, and every trade decided before that moment must come out identical. If one moves, the file is rejected and the model is handed the trade that moved. That proves the code is causal — it says nothing about whether it makes money.
Writing costs you nothing against the trial ledger, and this tab deliberately does not show the validation run's P&L or t-statistic. Reporting them would make generation a free search over your data, which is the exact thing the ledger exists to count. Backtest it on the Backtest tab, sweep it on Optimize, and let the ledger price the result.
