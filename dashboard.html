Before you use this

The prop-firm rules in this tool are best-effort and dated. They were read from each firm's own published pages and every value carries the date it was retrieved. Firms change rules roughly quarterly and sometimes contradict their own pages — during research one firm had removed a daily loss limit entirely while its pricing page still showed it, and another's marketing page understated a payout cap by 2.5×.

Verify with the firm before paying for an evaluation. Values the tool could not confirm are marked UNVERIFIED and every account shows what the simulator does not model.

This is a simulator, not financial advice, and a simulated pass rate is not a prediction about your account.

Run a strategy over your own tick data, then score the result against a prop firm. Three choices define the run and all three change the answer.

These are PropSim's own strategies, not the ones in your NinjaTrader. They are written in Python and run here, on your tick tape — useful for testing an idea, and for the order-flow class NinjaTrader cannot backtest at all. Your NinjaScript (.cs) strategies cannot run here: they are C# and they execute inside NinjaTrader. They reach PropSim by two other doors — a Strategy Analyzer run captured by the add-on appears under Imported, and a Market Replay / Playback session appears directly in the trade-source picker on the Prop Firm tab. See docs/connect-your-strategies.md.

Fills are pessimistic on purpose: you pay slippage both ways, and when a stop and a target fall in the same moment the stop wins. Measured on real trades, this setting alone can move a result from convincing to worthless — so it is a dial, never a hidden constant.

Backtests of your own NinjaTrader strategies, captured by the companion add-on. NinjaTrader does not save Strategy Analyzer results to its database, so the add-on writes each run to a folder PropSim reads.

PropSim reads one folder. Everything else — your trades, your accounts, your instruments and their point values, your commissions — is derived from it.
